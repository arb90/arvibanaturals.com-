Our story

Herbs our grandmothers trusted

ARVIBA NATURALS began in a home kitchen in Lahore, grinding herbs for family and friends who wanted skincare without a label full of unknowns.

Simple, because skin prefers it

We keep our formulas short. A clay is just clay. A single herb stays a single herb. Our one blend, the ubtan, holds five ingredients and not a sixth. That restraint is deliberate — it means you always know exactly what has touched your face, and it makes it easy to spot what is working for you.

Everything is milled in small batches and sealed the same week, so the powders reach you fresh and fragrant rather than sitting in a warehouse for a year. We buy our herbs directly from growers and mills we have visited, and we would rather run out of stock than substitute a lesser lot.
